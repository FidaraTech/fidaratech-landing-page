Hosting

This website is hosted by a standard web hosting provider. Technical data such as your IP address, timestamps and browser information is processed in server logs for operation and security purposes. Where personal data is transferred outside the EU, transfers are based on the EU–US Data Privacy Framework and/or EU Standard Contractual Clauses.

Cookies & storage

This site sets no cookies and stores nothing in your browser's local storage or session storage. It does not use analytics or tracking.

Third-party content

The site uses Google Fonts (Inter, Manrope) and the AOS animation library served via a CDN. If you prefer no third-party requests at all, you can disable JavaScript or block external requests in your browser; the site remains readable.

Email contact

If you send us an email, the message and your email address are processed only to respond to your inquiry. Messages are deleted at the latest at the end of the university project (February 2027).
